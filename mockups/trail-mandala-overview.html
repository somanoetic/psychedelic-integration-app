Multitudes — Mandala Trail (overview)

Foundational regulation trunk at the base, 5 branches fanning upward, dashed marker-to-marker connections form the mandala.

Reading the picture. Three foundational Regulating markers form the trunk at the bottom. Above the fork, the trail splits into 5 branches (Regulating continuation, Parts, Belief, Somatic, Integration), each carrying its remaining markers. Dashed lines connect specific markers across branches — concept links, not track links. The crossing pattern is what makes the shape a mandala rather than a tree. Animation note for RN: tapping a branch should zoom/fade other branches and scale the chosen one into focus, with a "back to overview" affordance. This static mockup is the zoomed-out state.
