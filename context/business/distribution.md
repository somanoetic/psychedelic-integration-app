# Distribution & Go-To-Market — Multitudes

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-02
**Status:** Draft v1
**Parent:** `context/business/BUSINESS-PLAN.md`

> Distribution is THE bottleneck (cost-model.md proves unit economics work; the open question
> is reach). This plan is built around the founder's actual edge: a practitioner inside the
> psychedelic-integration community, in a niche the big wellness apps ignore.

---

## 1. The strategic premise

Do NOT compete with Calm/Headspace for "wellness" installs — you lose on discovery and ad
budget. Instead win a **small, motivated, underserved niche**: people in active psychedelic
integration (and the practitioners who serve them). The math (cost-model.md) says you don't
need hundreds of thousands of users — you need a few thousand of the *right* ones.

**Founder edge to exploit (this is the whole plan):**
- Real practitioner credibility in the exact niche.
- Existing professional network + community presence.
- A deep, already-built product (160 exercises, RAG, AI guide).
- A printed integration journal (Becky/Neil Hadfield's) as a physical funnel.

---

## 2. Audience segments (who, and where they already are)

1. **Self-directed integrators** — people who had an experience (retreat, ceremony, clinical
   ketamine, festival) and are now processing it alone. *Primary B2C buyer.*
2. **Practitioners** — integration coaches, therapists, retreat facilitators. *Word-of-mouth
   multipliers now; B2B buyers later.*
3. **Retreat centers / ketamine clinics** — serve cohorts of fresh-experience people who need
   integration support. *Channel partners + future B2B.*

---

## 3. Channels, ranked by fit (not by reach)

### Tier 1 — Founder-led, high-trust, low-cost (START HERE)
| Channel | Play | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Your own network** | Direct outreach to practitioners/clients you know; ask for trials + honest feedback | Warmest possible audience; seeds first reviews |
| **Practitioner word-of-mouth** | Give practitioners a free/comped account; they recommend it as self-directed homework (ADR-009 Pattern A/B — outside-app handoff) | One practitioner → many clients; compounds |
| **Retreat / clinic partnerships** | Offer the app as the "what now?" integration tool centers hand clients post-experience | Centers WANT an integration answer; you're it |
| **The printed journal funnel** | QR/insert in the physical journal → app; app → journal | Two-way funnel, already exists |

### Tier 2 — Community & content (build over months)
| Channel | Play | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Niche communities** | Reddit (r/RationalPsychonaut, r/Psychonaut, integration-focused subs), Discords, forums | Give value first; no spam. Founder-as-practitioner voice is credible here |
| **Content / education** | Turn journal chapters 1–14 (project_feature_education_surface) into public articles/SEO | Owned audience; ranks for "psychedelic integration" long-tail |
| **Podcast circuit** | Guest on psychedelic/therapy/IFS/polyvagal podcasts as a practitioner | Trust transfer; niche podcasts have devoted listeners |
| **Email list / waitlist** | Convert existing landing-page waitlist; nurture with integration content | You already have the waitlist + landing page |

### Tier 3 — Paid & scale (only AFTER conversion is proven)
| Channel | Play | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Targeted ads** | Meta/Reddit ads to interest-based psychedelic/wellness audiences | Only once you know LTV > CAC. Burns cash otherwise |
| **App Store Optimization** | Keywords: "psychedelic integration," "ketamine integration," "trip integration journal" | Cheap, slow, compounds. Low competition on these exact terms |
| **Influencer/creator** | Psychedelic-space creators | Expensive; defer |

---

## 4. Sequencing (don't do everything at once)

**Phase 1 — Seed (now → first 100 true users)**
- Personal network + 5–10 practitioner trials. Goal: real usage, testimonials, fix activation.
- Convert landing-page waitlist to a small paid beta. Learn the free→paid line.

**Phase 2 — Community (100 → ~1,000)**
- Show up in 2–3 niche communities as a contributor, not an advertiser.
- Ship the education content surface for SEO. Do a handful of podcasts.
- Land 1–2 retreat-center/clinic partnerships as referral channels.

**Phase 3 — Scale (1,000+)**
- Only here: test paid ads with measured CAC. Lean into whatever Phase 2 channel converted
  best. Begin the B2B conversation (separate doc).

## 5. The realistic target ladder (ties to cost-model.md §profitability)

| Milestone | Installs | ~Active subs @3% | Net/mo | Reachable via |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Break-even | ~1,500–2,000 | ~40–60 | ~$0 | Tier 1 + early Tier 2 |
| Side income ($2k/mo) | ~9,000–10,000 | ~280 | ~$2,000 | Tier 2 maturing + partnerships |
| Income replacement ($5k/mo) | ~22,000–25,000 | ~650 | ~$5,000 | Hard on B2C alone — pair with B2B |

**Honest note:** the jump from break-even to income-replacement is where pure B2C gets hard
for a solo founder. The practitioner/retreat relationships built in Tier 1 are ALSO the seed
list for the B2B path, which reaches $5k/mo with ~15–25 *customers* instead of ~25k installs.
Distribution work here does double duty.

---

## 6. What to measure (so you know which channel works)

- **Per-channel:** installs → activation (first journal/Huxley use) → trial → paid → retained.
- **Conversion rate** (free→paid) and **churn** — these decide which row of §5 you hit.
- **CAC by channel** before scaling spend; only pour money into channels where LTV > 3×CAC.
- The web admin dashboard (ADR-010) is where this lives.

---

## 7. Risks specific to distribution

1. **Niche is small** — caps pure-B2C ceiling. Mitigation: B2B + practitioner multipliers.
2. **Sensitive topic** — ad platforms restrict "psychedelic" targeting; some channels may
   reject ads. Mitigation: lean on organic/community/content where it's allowed.
3. **Trust is everything in this space** — one tone-deaf marketing move burns credibility.
   Founder-as-practitioner voice + wellness (not clinical) framing per ADR-009 is the guard.
4. **Solo-founder time** — distribution competes with dev time. Pick 2 channels, not 8.
