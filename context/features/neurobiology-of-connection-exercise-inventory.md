# Exercise Inventory — *The Neurobiology of Connection* (Natureza Gabriel, Hearth Science, 2025)

**Purpose:** A reviewable catalog of every concrete, do-able practice in the book, so we can
decide which to build into the app. Sourced from the full book text at
`knowledge-base/source-materials/Neurobiology of connectionc.md` (~15k lines).

**Status of the source:** The book is in the sources folder and extracted to markdown, but
**NOT yet chunked into RAG** — so Huxley currently cannot cite it. (Separate task; see bottom.)

**Why this matters:** This book's practices are almost entirely *unique* — an embodied,
"Health Creation Manual" organized as portals/doorways into the Connection System. This is
distinct from the borrowed Deb Dana exercises (Personal Connection Plan, Triggers & Glimmers,
Ventral Vagal Anchors) which come from *Polyvagal Exercises for Safety and Connection*.

The book's frame: wellbeing = keeping the **Connection System** online ≥51% of waking life,
via **portals** (rhythm, gravity, breath, face/voice, language, eyes, hands, touch, movement,
gesture, posture, heart) and **doorways** (safety, connection, intuition, relatedness,
enduring wellbeing). "Story follows state"; safety is a "sovereign decision."

## A. ORIENTING / MAP PRACTICES (front matter — the Autonomic Wheel)

1. **Twelve Primal Flavors of Self** — guided tour through all 12 autonomic states (Union,
   Restore, Enjoy, Connect, Play, Compete, Accommodate, Appease, Fight/Flight, Freeze,
   Placate, Shutdown), feeling each. *Find your "autonomic home."*
2. **Autonomic Cycles of Deep Time** — map the 12 states onto a year/clock face; see how life
   cycles through them.
3. **Locate Yourself on the Map** — identify your "autonomic home" (health-creating state you
   return to) and your "prison-away-from-home" (habitual defensive state).

## B. SAFETY — "Noticing" practices (Ch. 15, *Safety is a Sovereign Decision*)
A whole suite of self-inventory practices — strong candidates for a structured exercise:

4. **Noticing Safety Shifts** — track micro-body changes when you feel safer (shoulders, belly, breath, voice).
5. **Noticing in Time** — scan last day/week/month for moments of deepest safety: when/where/with whom?
6. **Noticing in Tone** — list your reliable bodily safety signals (singing, swimming, appetite, facial ease).
7. **Noticing in Place** — find the safest spot in your home; notice what makes it safe; modify spaces intentionally.
8. **Noticing in Nature** — discern vista vs. refuge preferences; read view-preference as autonomic cue.
9. **Noticing in Body/Movement** — feel how the body wants to open/stretch when safe.
10. **Noticing through Clothing** — armor vs. nest; how dress broadcasts safety/threat.
11. **Noticing through Objects** — which objects "talk to your hands" and create safety.
12. **Noticing through Relationships** — map who you feel safest with and why; name relational redlines.
13. **Tribe / Social-Location Inventory** — witness where you veil identity for safety (no judgment).

## C. PORTALS TO CONNECTION (Ch. 19–36) — the exercise core

**Gravity & Posture (Ch. 20, 35)**
14. Rest Into Gravity (feel weight/contact; soften into support)
15. Head-on-Neck Awareness / figure-8 with breath
16. Squat / wobble-stool / standing-desk (ancestral posture)
17. Feet-on-Ground sensing
18. Shoulders-drop / whole-body weight release
19. Let Bones Hold You Up (skeletal support, muscles drape)
20. Postural "bodysets of safety": free hips, soft belly, open chest

**Breath (Ch. 21)**
21. Surfing the Breath (attend without changing)
22. 4-7-8 breath (in 4 / hold 7 / out 8, ×4)
23. Earplugs Breath-Listening
24. Notice Yawns (attention potentiates oxygenation)
25. Notice Breath Pauses (fullness/emptiness → autonomic baseline)
26. Forest Breathing (tree/human gas-exchange reciprocity)

**Face & Voice (Ch. 22–23, 26)**
27. Making Faces in the Mirror (exaggerate expression; feel it in the belly)
28. Mirroring Expressions (with partner/pet; develop *Nunchi*)
29. Opening the Voice (loud vocalization in a safe place)
30. "If You Could Have Said Anything" (post-stress vocal release with a trusted partner)
31. Singing (vibrate the interior; shower-singing)
32. "Tell Your Face" (consciously let the face register the state you want)
33. Face-Play with Animals/Partners (snarl, eyebrows, mirror — 10–15 min)

**Language / Internal Dialogue (Ch. 24)**
34. Kind Internal Dialogue (the ANS doesn't get sarcasm; speak to the inner child)
35. The "Yet" technique ("I can't… yet")
36. Pronoun Ownership ("my brilliance" / "the anxiety")

**Eyes & Hearing (Ch. 25, 27)**
37. 36 Questions + 4-Minute Eye-Gaze (Aron protocol, with a partner)
38. Safe-Voices Playlist (curate sound for felt safety; read your spaces acoustically)

**Hands & Touch (Ch. 18, 28–30)**
39. Put Attention in Your Hands (flow awareness into hands anywhere)
40. Handwork from 3 domains (craft / nature / daily-living)
41. Knife-sharpening by hand (meditative tactile focus)
42. Two-Pushpin sensory-resolution mapping
43. Hand-Sensing over objects, eyes closed ("hands as radar")
44. Walking with hand-sensing (eyes open → closed → in the dark)
45. Palm-toward-living-things (sense a creature/plant; ask a tree permission)
46. Gentle skin caress / *cafuné* (emotional touch)
47. Self-massage + joint compression (Hilton's Law → ANS)
48. Weighted blanket / Brazilian hammock (compression + rocking)
49. Cold exposure / temperature variation / swimming-with-skin-attention

**Movement & Gesture (Ch. 30–31, 33–34)**
50. Slowed-Swallow awareness
51. Incomplete-Motor-Program tracking (chronic tension as held gesture)
52. Defense-Gesture recognition (notice + permission to complete the impulse)
53. Integration / Connection gestures (lean into settling & reaching-out impulses)
54. Victory gestures (fist-pump / arms-up to feed vitality)
55. Expressive hands-and-face talk (release inhibition)
56. Spontaneous responsive movement (move as the body asks)

**Heart (Ch. 36)**
57. Daily Gratitude Recall (beads-on-a-necklace before sleep)
58. Say "Thank You" often (heart-opening; HRV coherence)
59. Gratitude Visualization when agitated (survey + thank + go outside)
60. Receptive Gesture of Receiving (open palms, soften chest)

## D. INTEROCEPTION / INTUITION / GROUNDING (Ch. 31, 37–38)
61. Deep-Belly (hara/dantien) awareness — hand below navel, breathe into it
62. Interoceptive Discernment — separate "neurology" (sensation) from "chemistry" (mood)
63. Settled Interoception — turn inward *only when connection system is online*
64. Barefoot Grounding + Root Visualization
65. Three-Point Foot Grounding (heel + two forefoot points; squat)
66. Hip-opening / psoas release (trauma-informed, slow)
67. Energy-Perimeter mapping (assistant approaches; mark where you feel them)
68. Feet-as-Portal-to-Knowing (ask your feet questions; intuitive listening)
69. Intuitive Permission-Giving (act on the knowing you can't explain)

## E. RELATEDNESS & ENDURING WELLBEING (Ch. 39–40)
70. Cultivate ~25 "Storycatchers" (trusted people who receive your "story of the day")
71. Embodied Storytelling in Community (re-enact + complete trauma motor-gestures, witnessed)
72. Grieve-into-Love with community (don't grieve alone)
73. *Sawubona* witnessing ("I see you" — deep mutual seeing)
74. "Villaging" (seek micro-moments of relatedness all day — people, birds, trees, stones)
75. Relationship Reciprocity tracking (notice asymmetry; prune one-directional ties)
76. Outdoor Connection (go outside/open a window when unwell)
77. Tracker's Syllogism (greet & talk to things; start with the first morning bird)
78. **51% Connection-System Nourishment** — the book's master practice: keep connection
    online ≥51% of waking life; treat it as non-negotiable, like eating.
79. Dwell in Good Moments ("sip" a good feeling at the sensory level — cf. Dana's "savoring")
80. Selective Input Curation (intentional about media/people/environments)
81. Approaching the Hearth (receptive meditation: open palms to fire/warmth, become transparent to it)

## Build candidates (my read — for discussion)

The book is too big to port wholesale. Natural ways to surface it in-app:

- **A "Portals to Connection" practice library** — the single richest, most app-shaped idea:
  a browseable set of short embodied micro-practices (breath, face/voice, hands, gravity,
  gesture, heart), each a 1–3 min guided card. Maps cleanly onto the existing exercise/
  `tryThis` format and the guided-narration screen (see memory `guided-exercise-narration-v1`).
- **A "Safety Noticing" structured exercise** — items 4–13 are already a coherent self-inventory
  (time/tone/place/nature/body/clothing/objects/relationships/tribe). Form-shaped.
- **"Find Your Autonomic Home"** — items 1–3 as an onboarding/orienting reflection.
- **51% as a gentle daily frame** — not a streak (per `project_feature_curriculum_trail`);
  a "is your connection system fed today?" check-in.
- The book also deepens the existing `neurobiology_of_connection` Learn article — its metaphors
  (portals/doorways, sovereign safety, story-follows-state) could become a follow-on article.
