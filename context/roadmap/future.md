## Risk Factors

### Market Risks
- Changing psychedelic legality
- Competition from established apps
- User adoption challenges
- Monetization difficulty

### Technical Risks
- API cost scaling
- Platform dependency (Expo, Supabase)
- Security and privacy concerns
- Performance at scale

### Operational Risks
- Single developer dependency
- Time and resource constraints
- Scope creep
- Burnout

---

## Mitigation Strategies

**For Market Risks:**
- Build strong core value
- Focus on differentiation
- Listen to users closely
- Pivot if needed

**For Technical Risks:**
- Keep architecture flexible
- Monitor costs closely
- Security-first mindset
- Plan for scaling early

**For Operational Risks:**
- Sustainable pace
- Clear priorities
- Say no to scope creep
- Build community of contributors
