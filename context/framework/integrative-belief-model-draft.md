## Purpose

To develop a coherent meta-model that integrates CBT, IFS, polyvagal/somatic, predictive processing, memory reconsolidation, and schema therapy — and use it as the conceptual scaffold for how the app handles belief work, parts work, nervous-system regulation, and integration.

This is meant to eventually become a framework that *shows how these models come together* — not just for internal app design, but as a contribution in its own right. No formal paper unifying these exists yet in the literature.

---

## Core Claim

Lived experience is generated by a population of predictive models operating at different levels. Suffering arises when these models are mis-calibrated, in conflict, or carrying outdated priors from formative experiences. Therapeutic change occurs through **juxtaposition** — holding an outdated prior in simultaneous awareness with a contradicting lived experience under conditions of sufficient precision modulation.

---

## Architecture: 5 levels of predictive models

| Level | What it predicts | Native vocabulary | Update pathway |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Cognitive-narrative | What things mean, who I am, what's true | CBT thoughts, core beliefs, schemas | Verbal reflection, cognitive restructuring (weakest pathway alone) |
| 2. Emotional-affective | What I feel about X | Affect labels, emotion regulation | Naming, witnessing, affective resonance |
| 3. Parts / sub-personality | What part of me believes what | IFS parts, schema modes | Parts dialogue, unburdening |
| 4. Autonomic-somatic | Is this safe? What state should I be in? | Polyvagal states (ventral/sympathetic/dorsal), interoception | Co-regulation, somatic practices, breath, titration |
| 5. Perceptual-prior | What's actually out there | RAS, attention, Bayesian priors | Prediction error from lived experience (most direct) |

Under threat, cognitive-narrative (L1) goes offline first; autonomic-somatic (L4) and perceptual-prior (L5) keep running and *generate* what the higher levels then try to make sense of. This is why insight without somatic change fails.

---

## The mechanism: three ways a prior updates

1. **Precision modulation** — the prior's grip loosens (meditation, safe contexts, psychedelics, co-regulation). Necessary but not sufficient for change.
2. **Juxtaposition** — the prior is activated simultaneously with a contradicting lived experience (Ecker's TRP, imagery rescripting, IFS unburdening, EMDR, AEDP). This is the actual rewriting step.
3. **Repetition under varied conditions** — the new prior gets reinforced across contexts until it generalizes. Without this, juxtaposition produces "aha" moments that don't stick.

Every respected modality does some combination. CBT: (1) weakly + (3) well. IFS: (2) + (3) when embodied. Somatic Experiencing: (1) + (2). Psychedelic-assisted therapy: (1) strongly + (2) opportunistically; integration handles (3).

## The clinical sequence

```
1. STATE CHECK    →  2. SURFACE    →  3. ACTIVATE   →  4. JUXTAPOSE  →  5. CONSOLIDATE
   (autonomic        (which level   (the prior,      (with contra-    (repeat across
    regulated?)       holds it?)     somatically)     dicting felt     contexts over
                                                      experience)       time)
```

- **State check** — is prior-revision possible? Polyvagal ventral or "edge of activation" — not dorsal collapse or sympathetic flood. Psychedelics open this window pharmacologically.
- **Surface** — find the prior at the level it actually lives. Layer-mismatch detection happens here.
- **Activate** — bring the prior into present somatic experience, not abstract recall.
- **Juxtapose** — introduce the contradicting experience as embodied reality, not argument.
- **Consolidate** — reinforce through repetition, journaling, behavioral experiments, return visits. *This is where most app-supported work lives.*

## Intervention grid (DRAFT — needs validation against literature)

|  | L1: Cognitive | L2: Emotional | L3: Parts | L4: Autonomic | L5: Perceptual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **State check** | (precondition, not done at level) | | | | |
| **Surface** | Thought record, belief inventory | Affect labeling, emotion wheel | "Who in you believes that?" | Body scan, NS check-in | "What did you notice today that fit X?" |
| **Activate** | Articulate fully ("if true, then...") | Stay with feeling, intensify slowly | Direct dialogue with part | Bring state into present awareness | Recall a moment when this fired |
| **Juxtapose** | Evidence against, behavioral experiment | Hold feeling + contradicting felt-sense | Self-energy meets part's burden | Co-regulation, resourcing, somatic contradiction | New experience that violates prior |
| **Consolidate** | Cognitive rehearsal, journaling | Affective tolerance practice | Return visits to unburdened part | Repeated NS regulation in trigger contexts | Daily attention practice |

Open issues:
- L5 juxtapose cell is under-developed in any existing modality. Worth noting as a research gap.
- Some interventions (imagery rescripting, EMDR) span L3+L4 surface+activate+juxtapose simultaneously — the grid doesn't capture this multi-cell action.

## Decision tree: level-of-entry (DRAFT)

```
Is the user in a regulated state? (L4 check)
├── No (dorsal collapse or sympathetic flood)
│   └── ENTER AT L4. Regulate first. Nothing else works until this is true.
└── Yes
    ├── Is there a clear part-voice carrying the belief? (felt-sense, age, story)
    │   ├── Yes → ENTER AT L3 (IFS/parts work)
    │   └── No
    │       ├── Does the belief have somatic correlates that don't budge with reasoning?
    │       │   ├── Yes → ENTER AT L4 (somatic) then back up to L1
    │       │   └── No
    │       │       ├── Is the belief amenable to disconfirming evidence?
    │       │       │   ├── Yes → ENTER AT L1 (CBT-style)
    │       │       │   └── No (selective perception) → ENTER AT L5
    │       │       └── L2 entry is usually a transition, not a starting point
```

Confidence: low on the exact branches; high on the principle that *most apps and clinicians default to L1 entry, and most of the time that's wrong*.

## Where frameworks genuinely conflict (provisional resolutions)

### CBT vs. IFS — status of "distorted" thoughts
CBT treats catastrophic thoughts as cognitive errors to be corrected. IFS treats them as accurate communications from a part holding something.

**Provisional resolution:** CBT correct when belief is genuinely confined to L1 (no part-voice, no felt-sense, no developmental origin). IFS correct when a part is actively holding it. Diagnostic test: does the thought have a felt-sense, a developmental origin story, or a protective function? If yes → part. If no → L1 cognitive habit.

### Polyvagal vs. PP — status of autonomic states
Polyvagal: autonomic states are evolutionarily-organized response hierarchies triggered by neuroception (bottom-up). PP: autonomic states are predictions the brain issues about what state the body should be in (top-down, allostasis).

**Provisional resolution:** Both partially right. Neuroception is fast unconscious detection that *informs* the prediction; the resulting autonomic state IS a prediction. Update path: bottom-up safety cues (polyvagal route) or top-down reappraisal under safety (PP route). Most people need the former before the latter works.

### Memory reconsolidation vs. schema therapy — timing
Ecker: specific window (memory reactivated, contradicting experience within ~5hrs, sufficient mismatch). Schema therapy: less mechanistically strict; imagery rescripting workhorse but timing/mismatch looser.

**Provisional resolution:** Ecker more mechanistically precise; schema therapy more clinically forgiving. App can't enforce reconsolidation conditions but can *teach the user to recognize* naturally-arising juxtaposition moments and capture them deliberately.

## Three things still underdeveloped

1. **Theory of stuckness.** Why do some priors resist all interventions in their level's column? Precision-rigidity? Multiple-part-conflict? Trauma-induced isolation? UtEB has thoughts; defer until user reads.
2. **Theory of transfer between levels.** When juxtaposition succeeds at L3, does the belief update at L1 too? Clinically yes — but why? Likely because levels are views into one predictive system, and propagation requires lower levels online during change.
3. **Where psychedelics fit precisely.** REBUS says priors relax globally — but at which level most? Provisional guess: L5 dramatically, L4 heavily, L3 variably (dose-dependent), L1 only as downstream effect. Would predict: psychedelic insights "feel true" during experience (low-level priors quiet) but fade unless juxtaposition delivered. Matches clinical observation. Pressure-test against REBUS.

## Decision: app release vs. model development

**The question:** Should the app launch as-is, or is this framework foundational enough that the app should wait for it?

**Provisional view (to be discussed with user):** The current app already embodies *parts* of this framework — IFS work, NS check-ins, Core Beliefs Inventory, integration journaling all exist. They don't yet talk to each other through a unified model, but they aren't *wrong* — they're just not yet integrated. Launching as-is doesn't undermine the eventual framework; the framework can land as a v2 organizing layer over existing features.

**Counter-argument:** If the framework genuinely changes which interventions get used when (e.g., gating belief work behind NS-state checks), then the current app may guide users into ineffective patterns (cognitive-only work when somatic entry is needed). That's not catastrophic, but it's worth being honest about.

**Recommended:** Launch the app on its current trajectory. Treat the framework as the organizing model for v2 (or for a "Practitioner Edition" feature set). Do not block release.
