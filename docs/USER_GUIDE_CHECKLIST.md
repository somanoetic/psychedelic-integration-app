# Session Preparation Checklist - User Guide

**Welcome to your Session Preparation Checklist!**

This guide will help you use the checklist feature to prepare thoroughly and confidently for your psychedelic sessions.

---

## What is the Session Preparation Checklist?

The Session Preparation Checklist is a comprehensive tool that helps you remember and track all the important steps before a psychedelic experience. Think of it as your personal preparation assistant - ensuring nothing important gets forgotten during this vulnerable time.

### Why Use a Checklist?

Preparing for a psychedelic session can be overwhelming. A checklist helps you:

- **Reduce pre-session anxiety** - Know you haven't missed anything important
- **Stay organized** - All preparation steps in one place
- **Track your progress** - See how ready you are at a glance
- **Build confidence** - Feel prepared and supported

### What's Included?

Each checklist contains **18 carefully-researched preparation items** organized into 4 categories:

1. **Physical Preparation** - Fasting, hydration, sleep, nutrition
2. **Safety & Support** - Sitter arrangements, emergency contacts, harm reduction
3. **Mental/Emotional** - Intentions, journaling, meditation, openness
4. **Practical Logistics** - Space preparation, supplies, music, schedule

---

## Getting Started

### Accessing Your Checklist

1. Open the app and navigate to your session
2. Tap **"Session Checklist"** or **"Preparation Checklist"**
3. Your checklist will load automatically

**First time?** The checklist will be created from our default template with all 18 items ready for you.

**Already started?** Your progress is saved automatically - pick up right where you left off.

---

## Using the Checklist

### Checking Off Items

As you complete each preparation step:

1. Tap the **checkbox** next to the item
2. The item will be marked complete with a checkmark
3. Your progress bar updates automatically

**Changed your mind?** Tap the checkbox again to uncheck it.

### Viewing Your Progress

At the top of your checklist, you'll see:

- **Progress bar** - Visual representation of completion
- **Completion count** - "12 of 18 complete"
- **Percentage** - "67% complete"

**Tip:** Aim for at least 80% completion before your session for the best preparation.

### Expanding Item Details

Some items have additional information:

1. Tap the **item title** to expand
2. Read the detailed description
3. Tap again to collapse

**Example:**
- **Title:** "Follow fasting guidelines"
- **Details:** "Fast for 4-6 hours before session (if applicable)"

### Essential vs. Optional Items

Some items are marked as **essential** with a special badge. These are the most important preparation steps based on research and best practices.

**Essential items include:**
- Fasting guidelines
- Hydration
- Sleep
- Sitter/guide confirmation
- Emergency contacts
- Setting intentions
- Space preparation
- Phone on airplane mode
- Clear schedule

**Tip:** Complete all essential items if possible. Optional items are recommended but can be adapted to your needs.

## Customizing Your Checklist

### Adding Your Own Items

Want to add a personal preparation step? Easy:

1. Scroll to the bottom of your checklist
2. Tap **"+ Add Custom Item"**
3. Enter:
   - **Title** (required, max 200 characters)
   - **Description** (optional, max 500 characters)
   - **Category** (Physical, Safety, Mental, or Practical)
4. Tap **"Add Item"**

Your custom item appears at the bottom of the selected category.

**Examples of custom items:**
- "Smudge the space with sage"
- "Call my integration therapist"
- "Review my journal from last session"
- "Prepare crystals and sacred objects"

**Note:** You can add up to 50 items per checklist.

### Deleting Custom Items

Made a mistake or changed your mind?

1. Find your custom item
2. Tap the **trash icon** on the right
3. Confirm deletion

**Important:** You can only delete items you added yourself. Default template items cannot be deleted.

## Categories Explained

### Physical Preparation

Items that prepare your body for the experience.

**Why it matters:** Your physical state significantly impacts your journey. Proper preparation helps your body process the substance safely and comfortably.

**Key items:**
- Fasting (reduces nausea)
- Hydration (supports body processes)
- Sleep (ensures mental clarity)
- Avoiding alcohol (prevents interactions)

### Safety & Support

Items that ensure you have help and resources available.

**Why it matters:** Having proper support creates a secure container for your experience. Safety first, always.

**Key items:**
- Trip sitter present
- Trusted person aware of your plans
- Emergency contacts ready
- Harm reduction resources reviewed

### Mental/Emotional Preparation

Items that prepare your mind and heart for the journey.

**Why it matters:** Your mindset (set) going into a session shapes your experience. Clarity and openness invite deeper insights.

**Key items:**
- Clear intentions
- Emotional check-in via journaling
- Grounding practices (meditation/breathwork)
- Releasing expectations

### Practical Logistics

Items that ensure your environment supports your needs.

**Why it matters:** Your setting shapes your experience. A comfortable, organized, distraction-free space allows you to focus inward.

**Key items:**
- Clean, comfortable space
- Supplies within reach
- Supportive music prepared
- Phone off or in airplane mode
- Clear schedule (no obligations)

## Tips for Best Results

### Start Early

Begin your checklist **1-2 days before** your session. Some items (like avoiding alcohol) need advance planning.

**Timeline suggestion:**
- **2 days before:** Review entire checklist, plan timeline
- **1 day before:** Complete physical and practical items
- **Day of:** Complete mental/emotional and final logistics

### Take It Seriously

These aren't just boxes to check. Each item serves a purpose based on research and community wisdom.

**Ask yourself:** "How can I complete this item with intention and care?"

### Adapt to Your Needs

The checklist is a guide, not a rigid rulebook. Adapt items to fit your:
- Substance type
- Setting (home, retreat, ceremony)
- Personal needs and preferences
- Support system

**Example adaptations:**
- Solo session: Extra emphasis on safety items
- Guided ceremony: Coordinate checklist with facilitator
- Microdosing: Lighter preparation may be appropriate

### Use It Every Time

Make the checklist a ritual. Each session deserves the same care and preparation.

**Why repeat?** Even experienced journeyers benefit from the structure and peace of mind a checklist provides.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I can't complete an item?

That's okay. Note why you couldn't complete it and consider:
- Is there an alternative way to address this preparation need?
- Is it safe to proceed without this item?
- Should I postpone the session?

**Example:** Can't find a sitter? Consider:
- Virtual sitter via video call
- Trusted friend on standby
- Postponing until proper support is available

### Can I use the same checklist for multiple sessions?

No - each session gets its own checklist. This allows you to:
- Track preparation per session
- Adapt to different circumstances
- Review past preparation patterns

**Your previous checklists are saved** so you can review what worked well.

### What if I'm at 100% but still feel unprepared?

Completing the checklist doesn't guarantee feeling ready - that's normal. Consider:

- **Journaling:** Write about your concerns
- **Meditation:** Sit with the uncertainty
- **Support:** Talk to your sitter or integration guide
- **Postponing:** It's okay to wait until you feel more ready

**Remember:** Some nervousness is natural and even healthy. Complete preparation reduces anxiety but doesn't eliminate it entirely.

### Do I have to complete every item?

Aim for **at least 80% completion**, with all essential items checked if possible. Some optional items may not apply to your situation.

**Trust yourself:** You know your needs best. Use the checklist as a guide, not a strict requirement.

### Can my sitter or therapist see my checklist?

Not currently. Your checklist is private and only visible to you. Future versions may allow sharing with support people.

### What if I'm in a group ceremony?

Coordinate your checklist with the ceremony facilitator:
- Some items handled by facilitator (space prep, music)
- Other items your responsibility (fasting, intentions, emergency contacts)
- Custom items may include "Confirm ceremony logistics" or "Review group agreements"

## Troubleshooting

### My checklist isn't loading

**Try:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh the screen
3. Close and reopen the app
4. Log out and log back in

If issues persist, contact support with your session ID.

### My progress disappeared

**Don't panic!** Your progress is saved automatically. Try:
1. Refreshing the screen (pull down)
2. Checking if you're viewing the correct session
3. Restarting the app

**Technical note:** Your checklist syncs with our servers. Even if offline, progress is cached locally and will sync when reconnected.

### I can't add a custom item

Possible reasons:
- You've reached the 50-item limit (remove some items first)
- Title is empty or too long (max 200 characters)
- Description is too long (max 500 characters)

Check for error messages and adjust your input.

### An item is stuck as checked/unchecked

**Try:**
1. Tap the checkbox again
2. Refresh the screen
3. Restart the app

If stuck items persist, this may indicate a sync issue. Your data is safe - contact support.

## Privacy & Data

### What data is stored?

Your checklist data includes:
- Which items you've checked off
- Custom items you've added
- Completion timestamps
- Progress percentages

### Who can see my checklist?

**Only you.** Checklists are private and protected by:
- User authentication (login required)
- Database-level security (Row Level Security)
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS)

### Can I delete my checklist data?

Yes. Deleting your account removes all checklist data permanently. Contact support for data deletion requests.

### Does my checklist leave my device?

Yes - it syncs with our secure servers to:
- Save your progress automatically
- Enable access across devices
- Prevent data loss

**Offline mode:** If offline, checklists are cached locally and sync when reconnected.
