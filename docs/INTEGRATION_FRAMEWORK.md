# Psychedelic Integration Framework

## Overview
This app uses a two-mode system combining systematic experience documentation (Robert Johnson's Inner Work) with therapeutic integration (IFS, Polyvagal, CBT, Somatic).

---

## MODE 1: EXPERIENCE MAPPING
**Based on Robert Johnson's "Inner Work" - Dream Work Method**

### Phase 0: Preparation
- User gets paper and pen
- Prepares to document experience systematically
- Ready to write things down physically

### Phase 1: Gathering Elements
**Goal:** Comprehensive data collection from the psychedelic experience

**Process:**
1. **Broad strokes storytelling** - Let them tell the whole story without interruption
2. **Targeted detail extraction** - Go back through each element for more depth
3. **Category prompts** - Visuals, sounds, emotions, movements, textures, somatics, relationships, insights

**Writing is crucial** - Physical act of writing makes connections real

**Move to Phase 2 when:** 10+ elements gathered across all categories, user confirms "nothing else"

### Phase 2: Connecting to Inner Dynamics
**Goal:** Connect each element to specific inner parts/dynamics/patterns

**Core Questions:**
- "What part of me is that?"
- "Where have I seen it functioning in my life lately?"
- "Where do I see that same trait in my personality?"
- "Who is it, inside me, who feels like that or behaves like that?"

**Process:**
1. Go through each element one at a time
2. Describe its main characteristics
3. Find it in their inner life with SPECIFIC examples
4. Write down the connections

**Types of Inner Dynamics:**
- Emotional events (surge of anger, wave of grief)
- Inner conflicts (part wants X, part wants Y)
- Inner personalities acting through you
- Feelings, attitudes, moods
- Belief systems and values
- Patterns of behavior

**Move to Phase 3 when:** All elements connected to specific inner dynamics with concrete life examples

### Phase 3: Interpretation - Finding Overall Meaning
**Goal:** Synthesize everything into one unified picture

**Core Questions:**
- "What is the central message this experience is communicating?"
- "What is it advising me to do?"
- "What is the single most important insight?"

**Process:**
1. **Synthesize** - Look at all elements and dynamics, find the overall story
2. **Write it out** - CRITICAL - brings it from fantasy to concrete
3. **State the one main idea** - Distill to essence
4. **Make it applicable** - What needs to change? How to live differently?

**Validation Principles:**
1. Choose interpretation that shows something NEW
2. Avoid ego inflation or self-congratulation
3. Avoid shifting responsibility away from yourself
4. Be willing to live with it over time if ambiguous

**Move to Phase 4 when:** Clear, coherent interpretation written out that passes validation principles

### Phase 4: Rituals - Making it Physical
**Goal:** DO something physical to honor the experience and integrate it

**Why Ritual Matters:**
- Takes understanding off abstract level
- Makes it concrete and immediate
- Registers at deepest levels of psyche
- "Transplants" awareness back into unconscious
- "Do something with your muscles!" - Toni Wolffe

**Types of Physical Acts:**

**Practical Acts:**
- Pay bills on time (if showed need for responsibility)
- Straighten out relationship
- Start exercise routine
- Change specific behavior

**Symbolic Rituals:**
- Bury "junk food" with ceremony (renunciation)
- Cast flowers into ocean (offering back to unconscious)
- Write letter to part of self and mail it
- Visit actual location from experience
- Look at bark of ten trees (embodiment)

**Ritual Principles:**
- **Small and subtle** - not grand or dramatic
- **Physical** - involving body and senses
- **Solitary** - private, not involving others
- **Silent** - not talking about it
- These register most deeply with unconscious

**Common Sense Warnings:**
- Don't confront others
- Don't act out destructively
- Don't use as license for bad behavior
- Keep responsibility with yourself
- If you can't think of anything, do SOMETHING simple

**Bridge to Therapeutic Integration:** The ritual becomes entry point for deeper therapeutic work

## MODE 2: THERAPEUTIC INTEGRATION
**Combines IFS, Polyvagal Theory, CBT, and Somatic Practices**

### Phase 0: Ritual Reflection & Bridge
**Goal:** Bridge from experience mapping to therapeutic work

**Process:**
1. "How did your ritual go?"
2. "What did you notice when you made it physical?"
3. "What came up for you?"
4. Use reflections to identify therapeutic themes
5. Assess readiness for deeper integration work

**Move to Phase 1 when:** Ritual complete, therapeutic themes identified

### Phase 1: Nervous System Assessment (Polyvagal)
**Goal:** Understand autonomic nervous system patterns

**Polyvagal Theory Framework:**
- **Ventral Vagal** (Safe & Social) - calm, connected, engaged
- **Sympathetic** (Fight/Flight) - activated, anxious, overwhelmed
- **Dorsal Vagal** (Shutdown/Freeze) - numb, disconnected, collapsed

**Assessment Process:**
1. Check current NS state from language/presentation
2. Map window of tolerance
3. Identify patterns from psychedelic experience
4. Connect NS dysregulation to life patterns
5. Offer regulation practices as needed

**Practices:**
- Calming breath (4-4-6 count)
- Polyvagal mapping exercise
- Grounding (5-4-3-2-1)
- Gentle activation (if shutdown)

**Move to Phase 2 when:** NS regulated enough for parts work, patterns identified

### Phase 2: Parts Work (IFS - Internal Family Systems)
**Goal:** Explore and heal relationships between inner parts

**IFS Framework:**
- **Exiles** - Wounded parts carrying pain/trauma
- **Managers** - Parts that try to keep exiles suppressed
- **Firefighters** - Emergency parts that distract from exile pain
- **Self** - Core consciousness, compassionate witness

**Process:**
1. Identify parts that showed up in psychedelic experience
2. Ask each part: What's your role? What do you need?
3. Notice conflicts between parts
4. Build relationship between Self and parts
5. Unburden exiles, appreciate protectors
6. Integration of shadow/disowned aspects

**Practices:**
- Parts check-in meditation
- Dialogue with specific part
- Asking permission from protectors
- Unburdening ritual for exiles

**Move to Phase 3 when:** Key parts identified, healing begun, patterns clear

### Phase 3: Pattern Recognition & Reframing (CBT)
**Goal:** Identify and reframe limiting beliefs and patterns

**CBT Components:**
- Identify automatic thoughts
- Recognize cognitive distortions
- Challenge unhelpful beliefs
- Reframe through new insights from experience
- Build new thought patterns

**Common Cognitive Distortions:**
- All-or-nothing thinking
- Catastrophizing
- Personalization
- Mind reading
- Emotional reasoning
- Should statements

**Process:**
1. Identify recurring life patterns shown in experience
2. Explore limiting beliefs underneath patterns
3. Connect to psychedelic insights about these beliefs
4. Challenge distortions with evidence
5. Reframe using experience wisdom
6. Practice new cognitive frameworks

**Practices:**
- Thought records
- Belief examination
- Evidence gathering
- Reframing exercises
- Behavioral experiments

**Move to Phase 4 when:** Patterns clear, reframes established, ready for ongoing practice

### Phase 4: Embodied Integration Practices
**Goal:** Sustain integration through ongoing somatic practices

**Components:**
- **Somatic Practices** - Body scans, felt sense, trauma release
- **Ongoing Rituals** - Daily practices to embody insights
- **Regular Check-ins** - NS state, parts dialogue, belief reinforcement
- **Habit Building** - Sustainable integration routines
- **Living the Insights** - Practical application in daily life

**Practices:**
- Integration body scan
- Daily mindfulness/meditation
- Journaling practice
- Movement/yoga/dance
- Creative expression
- Nature connection
- Community/relationship practices

**Ongoing Support:**
- Weekly/bi-weekly check-ins
- Practice refinement
- Addressing obstacles
- Celebrating progress
- Deepening insights over time

## KEY PRINCIPLES

1. **Write things down** - Physical act crucial for integration
2. **Small rituals > grand gestures** - Subtle is powerful
3. **Body wisdom** - Somatic experience essential
4. **Parts have wisdom** - All aspects serve a purpose
5. **NS state matters** - Work within window of tolerance
6. **Take it inward** - Experience as mirror of inner life
7. **Responsibility with self** - Not about blaming others
8. **Live the insights** - Truth lost unless embodied
