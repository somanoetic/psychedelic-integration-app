### FEAT-316-A: Expressive Writing Protocol (Pennebaker Method)
**Evidence:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (20+ years research)
**Effort:** 2 weeks
**Research:** Pennebaker's expressive writing paradigm - proven mental/physical health benefits

**Implementation:**
- 4-day protocol (15-20 min/day)
- Specific prompts about traumatic/difficult experiences
- Progressive depth exploration
- Integration-specific adaptations

**Benefits:**
- Reduced PTSD symptoms
- Improved immune function
- Better integration outcomes

**Priority:** HIGH - Quick win with massive research backing

### FEAT-321-A: Integration Timeline Protocol
**Evidence:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Clinical consensus, Gorman et al. 2021)
**Effort:** 2 weeks
**Research:** Evidence-based integration phases

**Implementation:**
- Immediate (0-24 hours): Rest, hydration, grounding
- Acute (1-7 days): Processing, journaling, support
- Short-term (1-4 weeks): Meaning-making, behavioral changes
- Long-term (1+ months): Sustained integration, lifestyle
- Automated timeline guidance

**Benefits:**
- Structured expectations
- Reduced confusion
- Phase-appropriate support

**Priority:** HIGH - Core to app mission

## ✅ Quick Reference: Feature Prioritization

### By Effort (Quick Wins)
- FEAT-316-B: Gratitude Journaling (1 week)
- FEAT-320-B: Loving-Kindness Meditation (1 week)
- FEAT-319-D: Progressive Muscle Relaxation (1-2 weeks)
- FEAT-320-C: RAIN Protocol (1-2 weeks)

### By Evidence (Highest Research Backing)
1. MBSR (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ thousands of studies)
2. CBM (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ strong clinical evidence)
3. Expressive Writing (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 20+ years research)
4. HRV Biometrics (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ gold standard)
5. EMDR/Bilateral Stim (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ extensive trauma research)

### By Impact (User Benefit)
- High: CBM, MBSR, Expressive Writing, Integration Timeline
- Medium-High: EMDR, Polyvagal Exercises, EMBARK, Expanded Subliminal
- Medium: IFS Chair Work, Schema Therapy, Somatic Experiencing

### By Complexity (Lowest to Highest)
- Low: Gratitude, RAIN, PMR, Loving-Kindness
- Medium: Expressive Writing, Timeline, EMBARK, Chair Work
- High: CBM, MBSR, EMDR, Somatic Experiencing
- Very High: HRV Biometrics
