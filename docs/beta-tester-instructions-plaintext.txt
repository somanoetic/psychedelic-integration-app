MULTITUDES BETA — TESTER INSTRUCTIONS

Thanks for helping test Multitudes! It's an early beta, so please expect some rough edges — that's exactly what we're looking for feedback on.

GETTING THE APP (iPhone/iPad only for now)
1. If you don't already have it, install the TestFlight app from the App Store.
2. Open this link on your iPhone/iPad: https://testflight.apple.com/join/RamyyGus
3. Tap Accept, then Install. The app will show up on your home screen as Multitudes.

You'll get a notification through TestFlight whenever there's an update — no need to check the App Store, updates come through automatically.

FIRST TIME OPENING THE APP
You'll need to create an account (email + password). This is a separate account from anything else — there's no "sign in with Apple/Google" yet.
You'll be asked to confirm your age and accept the (draft) Terms and Privacy Policy — this is expected, the beta screen will say so.

WHAT TO TEST
There's no fixed script — use it the way you'd actually use it. In particular we'd love feedback on:
- The Huxley AI chat (general conversation, or any of the guided modes like IFS)
- Journaling and the daily check-in trackers
- Anything that feels confusing, broken, or slow

HOW TO REPORT A BUG OR SHARE FEEDBACK
For anything you notice — a crash, something confusing, a typo, or just "this felt off" — email hello@multitudesapp.io with:
- What you were doing right before it happened
- What you expected vs. what happened
- A screenshot, if you can

A COUPLE OF THINGS TO KNOW
This is a wellness/reflection tool, not a clinical or emergency service. If you're ever in crisis, the app has a "Find Support" option with real crisis lines (988, Crisis Text Line, etc.) — please use those, not the app, in an emergency.
It's free during the beta — nothing to pay for or subscribe to.
Your data is real (not a sandbox) but this is still early software — don't rely on it as your only copy of anything important yet.

Thank you for testing!
