# Multitudes Beta — Tester Instructions

Thanks for helping test Multitudes! It's an early beta, so please expect some
rough edges — that's exactly what we're looking for feedback on.

## Getting the app (iPhone/iPad only for now)

1. If you don't already have it, install the **TestFlight** app from the App Store.
2. Open this link on your iPhone/iPad: **https://testflight.apple.com/join/RamyyGus**
3. Tap **Accept**, then **Install**. The app will show up on your home screen as
   **Multitudes**.

You'll get a notification through TestFlight whenever there's an update — no
need to check the App Store, updates come through automatically.

## First time opening the app

- You'll need to create an account (email + password). This is a separate account
  from anything else — there's no "sign in with Apple/Google" yet.
- You'll be asked to confirm your age and accept the (draft) Terms and Privacy
  Policy — this is expected, the beta screen will say so.

## What to test

There's no fixed script — use it the way you'd actually use it. In particular
we'd love feedback on:
- The Huxley AI chat (general conversation, or any of the guided modes like IFS)
- Journaling and the daily check-in trackers
- Anything that feels confusing, broken, or slow

## How to report a bug or share feedback

For anything you notice — a crash, something confusing, a typo, or just "this
felt off" — email **hello@multitudesapp.io** with:
- What you were doing right before it happened
- What you expected vs. what happened
- A screenshot, if you can

## A couple of things to know

- **This is a wellness/reflection tool, not a clinical or emergency service.**
  If you're ever in crisis, the app has a "Find Support" option with real crisis
  lines (988, Crisis Text Line, etc.) — please use those, not the app, in an
  emergency.
- It's free during the beta — nothing to pay for or subscribe to.
- Your data is real (not a sandbox) but this is still early software — don't
  rely on it as your only copy of anything important yet.

Thank you for testing!
